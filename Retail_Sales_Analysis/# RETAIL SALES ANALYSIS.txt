**8. Business Insights and Recommendations**

**Insight 1: Gender-wise Revenue Contribution**

Observation: Female customers give about half of the total revenue.

Meaning: Women customers are important for our sales.

Recommendation: We should make offers for female customers.

**Insight 2: Age Group-wise Revenue Analysis**

Observation: The 61–80 age group generates the highest revenue.

Meaning: People in this age range spend more money.

Recommendation: We can make offers or discounts for that age group.

**Insight 3: Top Revenue-Generating Product Category**

Observation: Electronics generates the highest revenue.

Meaning: Customers spend more money on electronics compared to other categories.

Recommendation: Business should keep enough stock of electronics.

**Insight 4: Monthly Revenue Trend**

Observation: May has the highest revenue, while September has the lowest revenue in 2023.

Meaning: Sales revenue changes from month to month.

Recommendation: The business should plan special offers during months with low sales.

**Insight 5: Product Category-wise Quantity**

Observation: Clothing has the highest quantity sold.

Meaning: Customers purchase more clothing products compared to other categories.

Recommendation: The business should maintain sufficient clothing stock.

**Insight 6: Gender-wise Quantity Sold**

Observation: Female customers purchase more products than male customers.

Meaning: Female customers contribute slightly more to the total quantity sold.

Recommendation: The business should understand the shopping preferences of both groups.
