# RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS – BUSINESS INSIGHTS

## 1. Gender-wise Revenue Contribution

**Observation:** Female customers give about half of the total revenue.

**Meaning:** Women customers are important for our sales.

**Recommendation:** We should make offers for female customers.

## 2. Age Group-wise Revenue Analysis

**Observation:** The 61–80 age group generates the highest revenue.

**Meaning:** People in this age range spend more money.

**Recommendation:** We can make offers or discounts for that age group.

## 3. Top Revenue-Generating Product Category

**Observation:** Electronics generates the highest revenue.

**Meaning:** Customers spend more money on electronics compared to other categories.

**Recommendation:** Business should keep enough stock of electronics.

## 4. Monthly Revenue Trend

**Observation:** May has the highest revenue, while September has the lowest revenue.

**Meaning:** Sales revenue changes from month to month.

**Recommendation:** The business should plan special offers during months with low sales.

## 5. Monthly Quantity Sold

**Observation:** The number of products sold changes every month.

**Meaning:** Customer demand is different in different months.

**Recommendation:** The business should maintain enough stock according to monthly demand.

## 6. Product Category-wise Quantity Sold

**Observation:** Different product categories have different sales quantities.

**Meaning:** Customers do not buy the same number of products from every category.

**Recommendation:** The business should maintain stock according to product demand.

## 7. Top 5 Customers by Revenue

**Observation:** A small group of customers contributes a significant amount of revenue.

**Meaning:** Some customers spend more money than others.

**Recommendation:** The business should provide special offers to loyal customers.

## 8. Revenue Distribution by Gender

**Observation:** Female and male customers contribute to total revenue.

**Meaning:** Both genders are important for business sales.

**Recommendation:** The business should create marketing offers for both groups.

## 9. Revenue Distribution by Product Category

**Observation:** Revenue is distributed across different product categories.

**Meaning:** Each category contributes to total business revenue.

**Recommendation:** The business should focus on high-performing categories and improve low-performing categories.

## 10. Correlation Analysis

**Observation:** Numerical variables show different relationships with each other.

**Meaning:** Some business factors may be related to sales performance.

**Recommendation:** The business should study these relationships to make better decisions.

---

## Conclusion

This Retail Sales Analysis project helped us understand customer behaviour, sales trends, product performance, and revenue distribution. These insights can help businesses make better decisions and improve sales performance.
